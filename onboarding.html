Grab Anything

If you can see it, you can save it.

1

Right-click anything

Images download at the best quality the site has — not the shrunken thumbnail. Text offers the font used. Logos save as crisp SVG files. Menus, heroes and footers save as working design files.

2

Point and grab with the picker

Press Alt+Shift+G (or right-click → Pick what to grab). Move your mouse to highlight anything, scroll to widen the selection, click to save it as an image or a design file.

3

See everything a page loaded

Pin the toolbar icon, then click it on any page: every image, video, and font the page fetched — download one, or all of them as a single ZIP. Plus one-click color palette and font cards.

Try it live on a photo-heavy page →
Everything runs on your device — no accounts, no tracking, nothing leaves your machine. Protected video streams (YouTube, Netflix) can't be saved; that's a browser-wide rule, not a missing feature.
